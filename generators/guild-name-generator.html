About Guild Names

Guild names are branding. A merchant house wants to sound solid and old; a thieves guild wants a name that means something to insiders and nothing to the watch.

Naming Patterns

  • The Colour Object — The Silver Hand, The Ashen Circle
  • Trade houses — a family name plus a trade word
  • Deniable names — criminal guilds prefer something dull

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I name a guild in D&D?▼
The Colour plus Object shape is the workhorse — The Crimson Ledger, The Ashen Circle. It sounds organised and implies a symbol.
Should a thieves guild sound criminal?▼
Rarely. A real criminal organisation wants a name it can say in public, so dull or commercial-sounding names are more believable.
What makes a merchant house sound old?▼
Family names, the word House, and a trade reference. House Varrow and Sons reads as three generations deep.
How do arcane orders differ?▼
They lean on abstract concepts and numbers — circles, chapters, degrees — implying hierarchy and hidden knowledge.
