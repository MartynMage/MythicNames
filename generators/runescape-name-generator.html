About Gielinor Naming

RuneScape splits neatly in two. In-world NPC names lean on plain medieval English — Duke Horacio, Hans, Bob — and stay deliberately unglamorous. Player names are a different species entirely, shaped by a 12-character limit and two decades of convention.

The regional cultures have their own logic. Fremennik names are Norse. Gnome names are comic and multisyllabic. Dwarven names lean Germanic with a mining bent.

The Patterns

  • NPCs — plain, medieval, unpretentious
  • Player names — short, punchy, within the character limit
  • Fremennik — Norse roots, patronymic endings
  • Gnome — long, silly, alliterative

MythicNames is an independent fan tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with Jagex. Names are procedurally generated and free to use.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these official RuneScape names?▼
No. This is an independent fan tool with no connection to Jagex. It generates original names in the style the game uses across its regions and NPC types.
Will a generated name be available in game?▼
Possibly not. RuneScape has a very long history and most short names are taken. Longer or more unusual combinations have a better chance.
What is the character limit for RuneScape names?▼
Display names are limited to 12 characters, which is why so much player-name convention involves abbreviation and creative spacing. Names here are kept within reach of that.
How do Fremennik names work?▼
They are Norse-derived, frequently with patronymic endings, matching the Fremennik Province's Viking-inspired culture. They stand out sharply from Misthalin and Asgarnian naming.
