About Vampire Names

Vampire names drip with aristocratic elegance and gothic mystery, reflecting Eastern European nobility.

Naming Traditions

  • Noble origins — Aristocratic, old-world feel
  • Eastern European — Romanian, Hungarian, Slavic roots
  • Titles of dread — Lord, Count, Master

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes vampire names sound gothic?▼
Vampire names have an aristocratic, old-world feel from Eastern European traditions.
Do vampires keep their mortal names?▼
Some keep birth names; others adopt new names marking their rebirth.
Should I use a title?▼
Titles like Count, Lord, or Baron add aristocratic gravitas.
How do I name an ancient vampire?▼
Use archaic names from dead languages or epithets like The Ancient One.
