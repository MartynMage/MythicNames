About Lost Ark Naming

Lost Ark allows 2 to 16 characters using letters, numbers and single spaces, and a name cannot start or end with a space. Every name here already fits.

Arkesia mixes Korean high fantasy with European romance, so names range from short and sharp to flowing and ornate depending on the class you pick.

MythicNames is an independent fan tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with Smilegate or Amazon Games. Names are procedurally generated and free to use.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long can a Lost Ark name be?▼
Between 2 and 16 characters, using letters, numbers and single spaces. A name cannot begin or end with a space.
Can I use special characters in Lost Ark?▼
No. Only letters, numbers and single spaces are accepted, which is why this generator produces nothing else.
Do names differ by class?▼
Not mechanically, but the classes carry very different tones. A Berserker name and a Bard name sound nothing alike, and picking the class first makes the name easier.
Are Lost Ark names unique per server?▼
Character names are unique within a region, so popular short names go quickly. Generating a batch and checking several is the practical approach.
