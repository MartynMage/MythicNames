About Tyrian Naming

Guild Wars 2 gives each of its five races a genuinely distinct naming logic, and they are among the most inventive in the genre. Asura take short, punchy names full of hard consonants. Charr use a given name plus a warband name, which doubles as a unit identity. Sylvari draw on Welsh and Irish sound patterns.

The Patterns By Race

  • Asura — brisk, clipped, often ending in a hard consonant
  • Charr — given name plus a warband name shared by the unit
  • Norn — Norse-rooted names with an earned epithet
  • Sylvari — Welsh and Irish influence, soft and green

MythicNames is an independent fan tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with ArenaNet or NCSOFT. Names are procedurally generated and free to use.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these official Guild Wars 2 names?▼
No. This is an independent fan tool with no connection to ArenaNet. It produces original names built to match each race's established naming style in Tyria.
How do charr warband names work?▼
A charr carries a given name plus a warband name shared with their unit, so the second name identifies the group rather than the family. Members of the same warband share it.
What makes asura names sound right?▼
Brevity and hard consonants. Asura names are short, often two syllables, and tend to end abruptly, which suits a race that considers everyone else slow.
Where do sylvari names come from?▼
They draw heavily on Welsh and Irish phonology, giving them a soft, green, slightly otherworldly sound that fits a race grown from the Pale Tree.
