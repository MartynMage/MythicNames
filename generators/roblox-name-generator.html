About Roblox Usernames

Roblox allows 3 to 20 characters made of letters and numbers, plus at most one underscore, which cannot sit at the start or the end. No spaces, no other symbols. Every name here already obeys all of that.

The one-underscore rule is the one people trip over. If a name you want is taken, a single underscore in the middle is usually the cleanest rescue — better than padding with digits.

What Works

  • Two words joined — the most reliable pattern on the platform
  • One underscore, in the middle — allowed, and it reads cleanly
  • Avoid trailing numbers — they date a name instantly
  • Say it out loud — friends read it in voice chat

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the Roblox username rules?▼
Between 3 and 20 characters, using letters, numbers and at most one underscore. The underscore cannot be the first or last character, and spaces and other symbols are not allowed.
Can a Roblox username have two underscores?▼
No. Roblox permits a single underscore per username, and it must sit somewhere in the middle. Every name generated here follows that rule.
How much does a Roblox name change cost?▼
Changing your username costs Robux. Because it is not free, it is worth generating a batch and settling on one you will still like in a year.
Are these names available?▼
No generator can guarantee availability, since millions of names are already claimed. Generate several and check the ones you like — longer names have far better odds.
