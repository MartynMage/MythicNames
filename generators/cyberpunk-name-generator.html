About Night City Naming

Cyberpunk splits names in two. There is the legal name on the corporate file, and there is the handle everyone actually uses. Which one you get told depends entirely on who is asking.

Corpo names are deliberately bland and international. Street handles are the opposite — short, loud and chosen to be remembered on a job. Nomads keep clan names because the clan is the only thing that persists.

By Role

  • Street — one punchy handle, no surname
  • Corpo — full legal name, forgettable on purpose
  • Nomad — given name plus a clan
  • Netrunner — a screen name that never matches a face

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a name limit in Cyberpunk 2077?▼
The game names its protagonist for you, so there is no field to fit. These names suit original characters, the tabletop game and fan fiction.
What is a street handle?▼
The name you are known by on a job, as opposed to whatever is on your corporate file. It is short, memorable and usually says something about what you do.
How do nomad names work?▼
A given name plus a clan name. Nomad clans function as extended families, so the clan half is the part that carries weight.
Why are corpo names so plain?▼
Deliberately. A corpo name is a personnel record, and blending into an international corporate roster is the point rather than standing out.
