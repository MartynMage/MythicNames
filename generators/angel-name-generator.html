About Angel Names

Angel names resonate with divine authority and celestial grace, often literally translating to their divine purpose.

Naming Traditions

  • Hebrew roots — Many end in -el meaning 'of God'
  • Virtue themes — Divine qualities
  • Musical quality — Harmonious, flowing sounds

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes angel names sound divine?▼
Angel names often end in -el or -iel (meaning 'of God' in Hebrew).
Can I use these for Aasimar?▼
Yes! Aasimar often use angelic names or names from their mortal upbringing.
How do different angel ranks have different names?▼
Higher-ranking angels have more complex, powerful-sounding names.
Should angel names be serious?▼
Traditional ones are reverent, but fantasy allows creativity with celestial patterns.
