About SWTOR Naming

SWTOR allows 21 characters including letters, spaces, dashes and apostrophes, which is generous enough for a full two-part name. Every name here already fits.

The Old Republic sits three thousand years before the films, so its naming is broader than the Star Wars most people know — Sith names in particular are personal names rather than Darth titles, because the Rule of Two has not happened yet.

MythicNames is an independent fan tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with BioWare, Electronic Arts or Lucasfilm. Names are procedurally generated and free to use.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long can a SWTOR name be?▼
Twenty-one characters, including letters, spaces, dashes and apostrophes. That is roomy enough for a full given name and surname.
Why are Old Republic Sith names different?▼
The game is set three thousand years before the films, before the Rule of Two. Sith at this point are a whole society with ordinary personal names rather than a pair of Darths.
Can I use an apostrophe in a SWTOR name?▼
Yes. SWTOR permits letters, spaces, dashes and apostrophes, which is unusually flexible for an MMO.
Are names unique per server?▼
Character names are unique within a server, so shorter names go quickly. Generating a batch and checking a few is the practical approach.
