About Fortnite Display Names

Your Fortnite name is your Epic Games display name. It allows 3 to 16 characters — letters, numbers, hyphens and underscores, with no spaces. Change it and every Epic title changes with it.

You can only change it once every two weeks, which is a good reason to pick something you will not regret. Every name here already fits the limit.

The Naming Cultures

  • Sweaty — short, sharp, usually one word plus a number
  • Clan-ready — a prefix or tag that survives being put in front of a team name
  • Clean — readable, no leetspeak, ages well
  • Funny — the name is the joke, and the joke lands in the kill feed

MythicNames is an independent fan tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with Epic Games. Names are procedurally generated and free to use.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many characters can a Fortnite name be?▼
Your Epic display name must be between 3 and 16 characters. It can contain letters, numbers, hyphens and underscores, but spaces are not allowed.
How often can I change my Fortnite name?▼
Epic allows a display name change once every two weeks, so it is worth settling on something you will still want in a month.
What makes a name look "sweaty"?▼
Short, aggressive words, often with a number or a doubled consonant. It is a genuine convention rather than a joke — the name is meant to read as fast as the playstyle.
Can I use symbols in my Fortnite name?▼
Only hyphens and underscores. Other symbols and spaces are rejected by Epic, which is why every name generated here sticks to the allowed set.
