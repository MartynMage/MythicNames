About Half-Elf Names

Half-elf naming is a question of who raised you. A half-elf brought up among elves carries a flowing elven name; one raised in a human town carries a practical human one; and many end up with both, or neither.

Naming Traditions

  • Upbringing decides — the culture that raised them supplies the name
  • Blended forms — an elven given name with a human surname is very common
  • Chosen names — half-elves who belong to neither world often pick their own

Frequently Asked Questions

How do half-elf names work in D&D?▼
Half-elves usually take names from whichever culture raised them. Some carry an elven given name with a human family name, which nicely signals a foot in both worlds.
Should a half-elf name sound elven or human?▼
Either works. Elven-style names are flowing and vowel-heavy; human-style names are shorter and more grounded. Picking one tells the reader something about the character.
Do half-elves have surnames?▼
Often yes, usually inherited from the human parent, since elven family names are far longer and tied to elven lineage traditions.
What if my half-elf was raised by neither parent?▼
A self-chosen name is a strong option, and a very common half-elf story. Wanderer-style names drawn from nature or the road suit that background well.
