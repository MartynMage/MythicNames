About Riot IDs

Your Valorant name is a Riot ID: a game name of 3 to 16 alphanumeric characters, followed by a tagline of 3 to 5. No symbols, no spaces, no underscores — Riot is stricter than most platforms, which is why names copied from other games often get rejected.

The same Riot ID carries across League of Legends and every other Riot title, so it is worth picking one that works outside a tactical shooter too.

What Works in Valorant

  • Short and sharp — it appears in the kill feed constantly
  • No symbols at all — the single most common rejection
  • Readable by a caster — if you ever play a scrim, someone says it aloud
  • A tagline that means something — three to five characters is enough for a joke

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the Riot ID rules?▼
A Riot ID is a game name of 3 to 16 alphanumeric characters plus a tagline of 3 to 5. Symbols, spaces and underscores are not permitted in either part.
Does my Valorant name carry to League of Legends?▼
Yes. The Riot ID is shared across Riot titles, so changing it in one game changes it everywhere. That is worth considering before picking something shooter-specific.
Why was my Valorant name rejected?▼
Almost always a symbol. Riot allows letters and numbers only, so underscores, hyphens and accents all fail — which is exactly why every name here sticks to alphanumerics.
What should my tagline be?▼
Three to five alphanumeric characters. Common choices are initials, a favourite number, a role, or a short joke that pairs with the name.
