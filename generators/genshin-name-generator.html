About Teyvat Naming

Genshin Impact assigns a distinct real-world naming tradition to each nation, which makes it unusually easy to build a convincing original character. Mondstadt is Germanic. Liyue is Chinese. Inazuma is Japanese. Sumeru draws on Persian, Arabic and Indian naming. Fontaine is French. Snezhnaya is Russian.

Get the nation right and the name will sit correctly in the world without any further effort.

The Nations

  • Mondstadt — Germanic roots, freedom-themed
  • Liyue — Chinese given and family names
  • Inazuma — Japanese naming, often with a clan name
  • Fontaine & Snezhnaya — French and Russian respectively

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these official Genshin Impact names?▼
No. This is an independent fan tool with no connection to HoYoverse. It generates original names following the real-world naming tradition each nation of Teyvat is based on.
Which real cultures do the nations use?▼
Mondstadt is Germanic, Liyue Chinese, Inazuma Japanese, Sumeru draws on Persian, Arabic and Indian traditions, Fontaine is French, Snezhnaya Russian, and Natlan draws on Mesoamerican and African naming.
Are these good for an original character?▼
Yes, that is the main use. Matching your OC's name to their nation is the fastest way to make them feel like they belong in Teyvat rather than being dropped into it.
Do Genshin characters have surnames?▼
It varies by nation. Liyue and Inazuma characters commonly have family or clan names, while many Mondstadt characters go by a single name.
