About Tiefling Names

Tiefling names reflect complex identity—caught between infernal heritage and mortal life.

Naming Categories

  • Infernal names — Exotic, demonic sounds
  • Human names — From surrounding culture
  • Virtue names — Chosen concepts like Hope or Fear

Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of names do tieflings have?▼
Tieflings have infernal names, human names, or virtue names they chose themselves.
What are tiefling virtue names?▼
Names representing concepts like Art, Despair, Hope, or Poetry.
Do tieflings have last names?▼
Tieflings rarely have family names; some adopt surnames or use single names.
How do infernal names work?▼
Infernal names have harsh, exotic sounds with unusual consonant combinations.
