About Dragonborn Names

Dragonborn names ring with ancient power and draconic majesty. Their culture emphasizes honor and clan loyalty.

Naming Traditions

  • Clan first — Clan name precedes personal name
  • Draconic sounds — Hard consonants, sibilants
  • Virtue meanings — Names represent ideals

Frequently Asked Questions

How do dragonborn names work?▼
Dragonborn have personal names and clan names, with clan names stated first.
Why does the clan name come first?▼
Dragonborn culture values the clan above the individual.
Do names relate to dragon ancestry?▼
Sometimes—a red dragonborn might have fire-themed name elements.
What do dragonborn names sound like?▼
