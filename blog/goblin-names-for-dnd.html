Goblin Names for D&D: From Silly to Sinister

Generate perfect goblin names for D&D encounters. Includes tribal naming conventions, boss names, and 100+ examples.

Goblins are a D&D staple, appearing in everything from first-level encounters to complex political plots. Their names range from the outright silly to the surprisingly menacing.

Goblin Naming Conventions

Goblin names reflect their culture:

  • Short — Usually 1-2 syllables
  • Harsh sounds — Lots of K, G, Z, and X
  • Descriptive — Often based on traits or deeds
  • Changeable — Goblins rename themselves freely
