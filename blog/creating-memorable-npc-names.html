Creating Memorable NPC Names: A DM's Guide

Published 10 January 2025 · Updated 14 August 2026

Learn how to create unforgettable NPC names that your players will remember. Tips, techniques, and 50+ ready-to-use names.

On this page

  • The Three Rules of NPC Names
  • Techniques for Memorable Names
  • Ready-to-Use NPC Names

Great NPCs need great names. As a DM, you'll create hundreds of characters over your campaigns — guards, merchants, rivals, and the barkeep behind every memorable fantasy tavern name. This guide teaches techniques for naming NPCs that players actually remember, and our human name generator covers the gaps when a session goes off-script.

The Three Rules of NPC Names

  1. Pronounceable — If players can't say it, they won't use it
  2. Distinctive — Avoid similar-sounding names in the same scene
  3. Memorable — Give important NPCs names that stick

Techniques for Memorable Names

Alliteration

Names that repeat sounds are easier to remember:

Barnaby Blackwood
Petra Pickpocket
Grumble Grimjaw

Descriptive Surnames

Tell players something about the character — the same sound-symbolism trick that makes villain names inspire fear:

Marcus One-Eye
Silent Sara
Old Ironbelly

The Nickname Trick

Give complex names simple nicknames:

  • Alexandros Nightingale → "Alex" or "The Nightingale"
  • Thorvaald Mountainbreaker → "Thor" or "Breaker"
